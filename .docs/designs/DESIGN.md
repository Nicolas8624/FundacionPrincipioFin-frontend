## Brand & Style

The brand visual identity pairs solemn reverence with transformative hope. It represents a threshold: the graceful closure of hardship and the luminescent dawn of new opportunities ("Podemos sanar y volver a comenzar"). Designed for benefactors, families, and individuals seeking deep emotional or physical renewal, the aesthetic communicates institutional gravitas, uncompromising dignity, and cosmic renewal.

The visual style merges **Refined Minimalism** with **Cosmic Glassmorphism**. Deep, unyielding voids of space provide boundless breathing room, punctuated by ethereal metallic gold transitions and luminous astral borders. Surfaces simulate weightless sheets of dark obsidian glass suspended in an infinite universe, framing human stories with warmth, prestige, and quiet sanctuary.

## Colors

The chromatic architecture is anchored in the cosmic infinite:

- **Cosmic Void (Neutral / Canvas):** Pure deep space black (`#000000`) for absolute background depths, graduating to abyssal obsidian (`#0A0A0E`) for structural containers and backdrop overlays.
- **Solar Renaissance (Primary & Secondary):** Warm imperial metallic gold (`#D4AF37`) transitioning linearly into incandescent pale astral gold (`#F5D77A`). This gold gradient is reserved for primary focal actions, fine borders, and key programmatic milestones.
- **Platinum Star-Dust (Tertiary):** Resonant platinum gray (`#C9CCD1`) provides subtle metallic hierarchy for subheadings, key metadata, and secondary interactive borders.
- **Typography & Text Contrast:** Pure celestial white (`#FFFFFF`) for primary titles and crucial metrics; diffused starlight gray (`#B8BCC4`) for body copy to ensure effortless, fatigue-free reading against dark glass backdrops.

## Typography

Typography balances monumental presence with intimate clarity:

- **Headings & Badges (Montserrat):** Always set in uppercase with expansive letter-spacing (`0.06em` to `0.18em`). This delivers an architectural, solemn rhythm that evokes classical inscriptions translated into modern minimalism.
- **Narrative & Prose (Inter):** Neutral, human-centric, and exceptionally legible. Set with generous line-heights (`1.6` to `1.65`) to allow emotional subject matter to be absorbed without visual friction.
- **Micro-accents & Hierarchy:** Numbers, timeline milestones, and programmatic categories use `label-md` and `label-sm` with gold linear treatments or platinum tints.

## Layout & Spacing

The foundation uses a 12-column responsive fluid grid on desktop (max content boundary: 1360px), dropping to an 8-column layout on tablet, and a single- or double-column structure on mobile devices:

- **Generous Voids:** Negative space is treated as an active emotional element mirroring cosmic infinity. Section spacing leverages `space-xl` and higher multiples to isolate poignant testimonials and donation actions without clutter.
- **Internal Component Harmony:** Card layouts maintain strict internal padding matching `space-lg` (2rem) on desktop, stepping down gracefully to `space-md` on compact mobile viewports.
- **Grid Alignment:** Elements nest against precise golden hairline rules, preventing visual drift and maintaining an orderly, comforting structure.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is expressed through astronomical layering, translucent glassmorphism, and radiant light spills rather than heavy physical dropshadows:

- **Level 0 (Cosmic Floor):** Deep pitch black `#000000`, occasionally overlaid with ultra-subtle radial gradient light orbs (diffused `#D4AF37` at 2%–4% opacity) to signify warmth and life.
- **Level 1 (Suspended Slabs / Cards):** Translucent glass surfaces utilizing `rgba(10, 10, 14, 0.70)` with an integrated `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`. Framed strictly by a crisp, 1px perimeter border tinted with a subtle gradient (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.35)` flowing to `rgba(245, 215, 122, 0.15)`).
- **Level 2 (Hover & Elevated Dialogue Modals):** Enhanced backdrop blur (`24px`), increasing container opacity to `rgba(10, 10, 14, 0.85)` coupled with a delicate celestial gold rim glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 25px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.12), inset 0 0 1px rgba(245, 215, 122, 0.40)`.

## Shapes

The design system incorporates a calibrated `roundedness: 2` (base 0.5rem / 8px radius) scale, with all primary content cards and structural glass modals explicitly anchored at **16px (`rounded-lg`)**. This curvature softens the solemnity of the deep void, providing a welcoming, humanizing embrace while avoiding frivolous hyper-circular motifs.

Action items such as tags, badges, and select impact counters scale down to subtle 6px or 8px corners, maintaining an architectural silhouette that honors the institution's noble purpose.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Cosmic Dawn):** Rich gradient background flowing from `#D4AF37` (left) to `#F5D77A` (right). Text is pure space black (`#0A0A0E`), uppercase Montserrat, weight 600, with letter-spacing `0.1em`. Subtle radial highlight on hover with `box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.40)`.
- **Secondary Action (Starlight Wire):** Transparent dark obsidian surface with a continuous 1px solid border in `#D4AF37`. Text in pure `#FFFFFF` or `#F5D77A`, shifting to gold-tinted fill (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.08)`) on hover.
- **Text / Astral Link:** High-contrast `#C9CCD1` body text underlined by an ultra-thin 1px gold hairline that smoothly expands from the center outward upon interaction.

### Cards & Story Containers
- Structured with 16px corner radius, `rgba(10, 10, 14, 0.7)` background, 16px backdrop blur, and 1px metallic gold border.
- Used for recovery testimonials, social initiative disclosures, and memorial donation tiers. Padding is standardized to 32px (`space-lg`).

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Inputs:** Obsidian surface (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)`), framed with a 1px border in `#C9CCD1` at 30% opacity. Upon focus, the border transitions seamlessly to a radiant metallic gold (`#D4AF37`) paired with an inner starlight diffusion (`box-shadow: 0 0 10px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.2)`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Minimal geometric squares and circles with 1px gold frames. Active state displays an internal glowing solar amber pip or checkmark.

### Badges, Chips & Milestones
- Dark obsidian base encapsulated with 1px `#D4AF37` at 40% opacity. Text is set in uppercase Montserrat (`label-md`) in `#F5D77A` or `#C9CCD1`.

### Iconography & Cosmic Glyphs
- Custom ultra-fine 1px or 1.25px stroke weight. Pure linear styling executed in `#D4AF37` or `#F5D77A`. Avoid solid or heavy silhouette fills; icons must appear delicate, celestial, and crystalline.
